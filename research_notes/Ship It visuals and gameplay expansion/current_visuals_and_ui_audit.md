## Layout and hierarchy: what is on screen, what draws the eye, how the space is split on phone and desktop

### Takeaway
One fixed five-block layout (readout, editor, key, tool panel, tmux-style tab bar) carries the whole game; on phone the tool panel gets about 37% of the screen and the editor 32%, and most panels need 3 to 12 screens of scrolling inside a 330px window. Desktop is the same four blocks rearranged into two columns capped at 1240px: it reads well at 1440x900 but shows one panel at a time and leaves 35 to 52% of the width empty on larger monitors.

### Inferences
- (Judgment) The hierarchy is clear and consistent: count first, key second, one panel third. The design comment's intent ("the game is a tmux session", [main.css:1-10](/home/user/ShipIt/src/styles/main.css)) is carried through faithfully.
- (Judgment) On phone the editor is mostly decoration (8 to 10 lines of generated code) while the panel, where every decision is made, is a 330px slit onto very long lists. The split favours atmosphere over the things the player reads and taps.
- (Judgment) Desktop is a rearranged phone layout rather than a desktop design: one panel at a time, a 444px panel column even on a 2560px monitor, and no use of the extra width for a second panel (for example pkgs beside ship), a persistent goal strip or a larger data view.
- (Judgment) The key shifting under the thumb when a script starts, and the hidden tabs at the right of the bar, are the two layout problems most likely to be felt in play on a phone.

### Gaps
- Not tested on a real phone or inside the Claude app's viewer (safe-area insets, the app toolbar, real touch).
- Only spot checks between 390 and 900px wide; no foldables or split-screen.

## Typography, colour and themes: fonts, sizes, contrast, palettes, colour as a state signal

### Takeaway
Everything is JetBrains Mono at 13 to 14px with one very large number; colour is used systematically by meaning (green lines, coral per tap, violet per second, gold stars, cyan names, red bugs) and that system is the strongest visual idea in the game. The weak points are low-contrast "dim" text (2.2 to 3.4:1), two of the four themes being single-hue, several hard-coded colours that break the light theme, and symbol glyphs that are not in the bundled font.
